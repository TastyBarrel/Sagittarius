What We Do

Three ways to work together.

Not rigid packages, but ways of engaging Sagittarius depending on what your organization needs right now.

01 · Understand

Organizational Assessment

When something is wrong and you don't know why.

The work can include mapping the organization and understanding structure and roles, interviewing leadership and employees (with balanced or random selection where it matters), and examining customers, products and services.

It can also mean looking at organizational history, culture, hiring, communication, meetings and processes, observing how work actually happens, and examining contractors, distributors and other external relationships. Where appropriate, that includes reviewing exit interviews and speaking with former employees, and triangulating what we learn across all of it.

The output is a clearer understanding of the organization and recommendations grounded in the evidence.

02 · Plan

Strategic Planning

When the organization needs direction.

The work starts with understanding what the organization is actually trying to accomplish, and the current organization and environment it's operating in.

From there, we develop a multi-year plan based on your needs, determine priorities, establish measurements and KPIs, and build a practical roadmap, then hand the plan back to the organization to carry forward.

03 · Continue

Ongoing Advisory & Implementation Support

When you want continued outside perspective or help putting recommendations into practice.

This can take the shape of recurring strategic conversations, implementation support, periodic organizational reviews, or ongoing advisory work more broadly.

You choose whether Sagittarius stays involved, and for how long. Our responsibility stays the same throughout: the quality of the inquiry, the understanding, and the recommendation.
