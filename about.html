About

Sagittarius started with one question.

It was asked inside a dozen different organizations. What's actually happening here, and why won't anyone say it out loud?

Our Vision

To see employers confidently operating successful, purpose-driven organizations.

Our Mission

We help employers understand what's actually happening in their own organization, why it's happening, and how to turn that understanding into positive change.

What to expect.

  • Led and delivered personally
  • An evidence-based investigation, not a guess
  • Solution-agnostic: the recommendation follows the evidence
  • Focused on root cause, not just symptoms
  • Honest, even when the answer isn't the one you expected
  • Grounded in your organization, not a template applied to it
  • Built to leave you more capable, not more dependent
  • A partner in seeing clearly, not just in fixing what's broken

What you won't find here.

  • A rigid, branded methodology
  • A predetermined solution looking for a problem
  • A validation service
  • A blame exercise
  • An exercise in proving you wrong
  • An excuse to make people uncomfortable
  • A generic strategy consultancy
  • Attractive reports for their own sake

Sagittarius - Latin for Archer

Excellence isn't a finish line you cross. It's a direction you hold, with the center in sight and a willingness to adjust when the arrow lands somewhere else.

Our work is helping organizations see where they actually are, so they can aim at what their founders imagined.

Founder & Principal Consultant

Paul Alderson

I've consistently found myself asking how an organization actually works, and where and why it fails. What employees see that management doesn't. Where stated values differ from actual behavior. How decisions affect the people who carry them out. How work actually gets done, and who really has influence.

I have worked across a wide range of sectors, including information technology, nuclear power, healthcare, national laboratories, pharmaceuticals, renewable energy, retail, and nonprofits. Each has different people, incentives, risks, and cultures, yet one pattern holds across all of them. The organizations that struggled most were the ones where purpose was either ill defined or disconnected from daily operations.

Sagittarius is the formal expression of how I've always thought about organizations. Today, I lead and personally deliver the work. Sagittarius isn't a firm with a bench you'll never meet.
