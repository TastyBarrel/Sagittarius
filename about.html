About

Organizations lose the ability to see themselves clearly.

Not usually because anyone is being deliberately dishonest, but because bureaucracy, hierarchy, fear, competing incentives, and habit filter what people are willing to say.

Our Vision

A future where employers see themselves honestly enough to become healthy organizations.

Our Mission

We help organizations understand what's actually happening in their own operations, why it's happening, and what needs to change.

Understanding what's actually happening means looking past what people say to what's really going on, and past the first explanation to the real one. Knowing why means tracing a problem to its actual source, not just its symptoms. And determining what needs to change means following the evidence to a recommendation, rather than starting with one and working backward to justify it.

What Sagittarius is.

  • A boutique practice, led and delivered personally
  • An evidence-based investigation, not a guess
  • Solution-agnostic: the recommendation follows the evidence
  • Focused on root cause, not just symptoms
  • Honest, even when the answer isn't the one you expected
  • Grounded in your organization, not a template applied to it
  • Built to leave you more capable, not more dependent
  • A partner in seeing clearly, not just in fixing what's broken

What Sagittarius is not.

  • Not a rigid, branded methodology
  • Not a predetermined solution looking for a problem
  • Not a validation service
  • Not a blame exercise
  • Not an exercise in proving you wrong
  • Not an excuse to make people uncomfortable
  • Not a generic strategy consultancy
  • Not in the business of attractive reports for their own sake

Paul Alderson

Understanding organizations isn't just a skill I've built over time. It's who I am, and it's shown up everywhere I've worked: retail, information technology, nuclear power, healthcare, national laboratories, pharmaceuticals, renewable energy, and nonprofit work. Seeing an organization as a living system was never in any of my job descriptions. I did it anyway, because I don't know how not to.

I've consistently found myself asking how an organization actually works, and where and why it fails. What employees see that management doesn't. Where stated values differ from actual behavior. How decisions affect the people who carry them out. How work actually gets done, and who really has influence.

Different industries have different people, incentives, risks, and cultures, but the same underlying questions keep repeating. Sagittarius is the formal expression of how I've always thought about organizations. Today, I lead and personally deliver the work. It's a boutique practice, not a firm with a bench you'll never meet.
