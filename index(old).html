Something isn't working.

You may know the symptoms. Maybe you're dealing with turnover, poor performance, stalled growth, customer problems, internal conflict, or a strategy that isn't producing the results you expected.

But knowing that something is wrong isn't the same as understanding why.

How we work.

We don't accept the problem as presented. We ask questions, listen to different perspectives, and examine the evidence before we accept anyone's explanation, including our own.

We look at the whole picture: leadership, employees, customers, data, and the way work actually happens. We form hypotheses, test them against what we find, and change our thinking when the evidence tells us to.

You'll leave the engagement with a clearer understanding of what's driving the problem, and why we believe our recommendations will make a difference. Not a report that sits on a shelf.

When something isn't making sense.

  • Something isn't working, and you can't figure out why.
  • You've tried different solutions and the problem keeps coming back.
  • Leadership and employees have very different ideas about what's going wrong.
  • The organization is changing, growing, or struggling to find a clear direction.
  • You know something needs to change, but you don't yet know what that change should look like.

These are the kinds of problems Sagittarius helps organizations work through.

We're not here to tell you what you want to hear.

We don't come in with a predetermined solution, and we don't assume that the first explanation is the right one. We follow the evidence, and change our thinking when it calls for it. The result is advice grounded in your organization, not a solution looking for a problem.

If you're looking for someone to confirm what you already believe, we're probably not the right fit.

You don't need to have the problem figured out before you call us. What matters is that you're willing to look honestly at what's happening and consider making changes based on what you learn.
